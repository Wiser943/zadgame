Submit your project

Have a working project? Send it to the AllConnect team for consideration.

What makes a strong submission?

  • A live URL that the review team can access.
  • A clear explanation of what players can do with it.
  • Mobile-friendly layout and reasonable loading time.
  • Transparent privacy, ads, payments, and data collection.
  • No malware, deceptive flows, or unauthorized collection of player data.
About developer payments
Choosing a monetization preference is not a promise of payment. Rewards are not automatic at submission. Any traffic-based program or revenue share must be approved separately, measured with platform-verified analytics, and governed by written eligibility, fraud, payout, and tax terms.
