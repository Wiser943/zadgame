A map that moves with people

Neighbours are tracked from the socket, interpolated on the client, and restored when you reconnect. You see them walking, not frozen on a spawn tile.

What you will see

Grass plots, asphalt, potholes, zebra crossings, zinc roofs and danfos — the same sprites used in Lagos Run — plus cluster badges when a venue is busy.

How to get around

Tap the ground to walk. Too far? Open transport. Filters at the top of Map hide go-slow, ads, sea plots or government sites.
