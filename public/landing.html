Questions before you enter?

Frequently asked

Do I need to download anything?

No. AllConnect runs in your browser on supported phones and computers.

Is AllConnect free?

Yes. You can create a character and start exploring without paying for a subscription.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Meet them in the city, chat, invite them to games, rematch and share between the connected apps.

Is Game Naira real money?

No. It is virtual in-game currency for jobs, rent, purchases and activities. There is no real-world cash-out.

What should I do first?

Start a character, finish your first shift, explore the map and open the Phone to discover the connected apps.
