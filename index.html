Inside the game

A whole life in the capital

  • ✓Create your resident: male or female, braids to gele and hijab, agbada to corper uniform, in Ankara, Adire and Aso-oke. Pick where you're from, your personality and your dream.
  • ✓Meet real people: everyone shares one Abuja. Chat by area, send private messages, form a crew, invite friends to your house or give them a lift.
  • ✓Work and earn: take 2-minute shifts at businesses owned by other players, or join the FCT Civil Service.
  • ✓Run a business: buy a real Abuja spot or start your own on a CBD plot. Set your prices, hire staff and pay tax.
  • ✓Find a home: rent in Nyanya, Lugbe, Kuje, Kubwa, Gwarinpa or Maitama, then build it up and furnish it.
  • ✓Get around: green-and-white taxis, along shared cabs, keke napep, the bus, or your own car. Fares follow the real roads.
  • ✓Eat, party, stay alive: suya, shawarma, IbroFish and more keep your hunger up. Dance at Club Zuma. Get sick or knocked down and it's the hospital.
  • ✓Mind the police: stop-and-search on the streets. Be rude or run and you'll spend time in a cell, or pay bail.
Run by its players

A city with a real economy and a real government.

In Abuja Life the businesses, the jobs and the government all belong to players. What you do changes the city for everyone.

Weekly elections

Run for Senator of your district or FCT Minister. Real players vote, and the winners hold office for the week.

A real treasury

Tax on every sale fills the CBN. The Minister sets the tax, funds the civil service and pays for good deeds. Every naira shows in the public accounts.

EFCC is watching

Officials who embezzle get audited, jailed without bail and made to pay it back, plus a fine.

Player-owned economy

Restaurants, shops, real estate firms and the club are owned by players. Wages and rent go to them, not to the computer.

Property and rent

Each district has a real estate firm a player can own. Renters pay it every month.

Your ideas, built fast

Suggestions from players go straight onto the board, and new updates land almost every day.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Abuja Life?

Abuja Life is a free multiplayer 3D life simulation game set in the real Abuja, Nigeria. You create a resident, arrive at the City Gate and build a life in the capital with real players: work, run a business, rent a home, make friends, party and take part in politics.

Is Abuja Life the Abuja version of the Lagos sims games?

Yes, you can think of it that way. If you've played Lagos Life or other Nigerian sims-style games, Abuja Life is the one set in the FCT: a real map of Abuja, a player-run economy and a government elected by the players.

Is it an Abuja Sims game?

It's a life simulation like The Sims, but set in Abuja and played online with other people. Instead of a single household you live in a shared city where other players own the shops, hire you, rent you a flat and run the government.

Is it free?

Yes. Abuja Life is free to play and you'll never need to pay to play. A few things are optional and cost a small amount of real money: topping up your in-game wallet (from ₦100), registering a business, standing for election and placing an ad.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in your browser on Android, iPhone and computers. Open abjlife.app/play and you're in. On your phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Is it really Abuja?

Yes. Roads and buildings come from the real map of Abuja, and the landmarks are built from photos. Distances and taxi fares are based on the real roads, and day and night follow the time in Abuja.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Everyone plays in the same Abuja. You can chat by area, send private messages, start a crew, invite friends to your house, send them money or give them a lift in your car.

How do I make money in Abuja Life?

Take shifts at a business owned by another player or at the FCT Civil Service, open your own business, own a district's real estate firm and collect rent, or win an election. Watch your spending: food, rent and transport all cost money.

Can I own a business?

Yes. You can buy one of the real places in the city or create a brand-new business on a plot in the Central Business District, with your own name, sign, items and prices. You hire other players and pay tax on every sale.

How do elections work?

Every week players can run for Senator of their district or for FCT Minister. Residents vote, and the winners take office. Officials spend the treasury on good deeds and palliatives, the Minister sets the tax, and anyone caught embezzling is arrested by the EFCC.

What happens if I get arrested?

Police do stop-and-search on the streets. Be rude, run or fight and you'll be locked in a cell for a few minutes. You can wait it out or pay bail, and bail goes to the government treasury.

Which places in Abuja are in the game?

The City Gate, National Mosque, National Christian Centre, Eagle Square, Millennium Park, Ceddi Plaza, Yar'Adua Centre, Magic Land, IbroFish, Club Zuma, Area 10 Market, the airport, universities and many more, with new places added often.

Does it work on older phones?

Yes. Abuja Life uses lighter graphics on small screens and slower phones so it runs smoothly. A good mobile data or Wi-Fi connection helps because the city is shared live with other players.

Is there a crypto coin or token?

No, and there never will be. Abuja Life has no coin or token. Be careful of anyone selling one in our name.

Who made Abuja Life?

Abuja Life is developed by Nandatech, a Nigerian software studio.

Who do I contact?

Email support@abjlife.app. You can also send ideas and bug reports from the Suggestions board inside the game.
